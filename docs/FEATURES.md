# AURA — Product Features and MVP Scope

## 1. Product Goal

Build an AI-powered student life companion that combines academic organization, healthy habits, practical life assistance, and supportive AI conversations.

## 2. MVP — Version 1

The first release will focus on a small set of connected, reliable features.

### 2.1 Account and Onboarding

- Student registration and login.
- Basic profile setup.
- University and academic information.
- Personal goals and preferences.
- Notification and privacy settings.

### 2.2 Home Dashboard

- Personalized greeting.
- Daily task overview.
- Upcoming academic deadlines.
- Daily habit checklist.
- Progress summary.
- Quick access to the AI companion.

### 2.3 AI Companion

- Text-based AI conversations.
- Supportive and helpful responses.
- Student-approved context and memory.
- Academic and everyday life assistance.
- Clear controls for conversation history and memory.

### 2.4 Academic Planner

- Add and manage courses.
- Create assignments and deadlines.
- Track quizzes, midterms, and exams.
- Calendar and study schedule.
- Task prioritization.
- Completion tracking.

### 2.5 Daily Habits and Wellness

- Water intake tracking.
- Meal and fruit reminders.
- Sleep routine tracking.
- Gym and exercise habits.
- Custom daily checklists.
- Flexible streaks and progress milestones.

### 2.6 Gamification

- XP for completed activities.
- Achievement badges.
- Habit milestones.
- Personal progress summaries.
- Optional streaks.
- Rest days and flexible goals.

Gamification must encourage progress without creating guilt, shame, or unhealthy pressure.

## 3. Version 2 — Personalization and Intelligence

- More advanced student memory.
- Context-aware daily recommendations.
- Personalized study suggestions.
- Smart reminder scheduling.
- More detailed progress insights.
- Improved AI conversations.
- Calendar integrations.

## 4. Version 3 — AURA Live

- Real-time voice conversations.
- Optional camera-based assistance.
- Cooking guidance.
- Laundry and clothing care assistance.
- Reading labels and instructions.
- Exercise guidance.
- Presentation practice.
- Voice-driven task management.

All microphone and camera features must be explicitly activated and controlled by the student.

## 5. Future Possibilities

- University learning management system integrations.
- Campus resource discovery.
- Wearable integrations.
- Local AI models.
- Advanced offline functionality.
- Multilingual assistance.
- Student community integrations, subject to privacy and safety review.

## 6. MVP Priorities

Priority 1:
- Account and onboarding.
- Student dashboard.
- Tasks and checklists.
- Basic habit tracking.
- Academic planner.

Priority 2:
- AI conversation.
- Student-approved memory.
- Reminder system.
- Progress and rewards.

Priority 3:
- Voice conversations.
- Camera assistance.
- Advanced personalization.

## 7. MVP Success Criteria

The first MVP should allow a student to:

1. Create an account and set up a profile.
2. Open a personalized dashboard.
3. Create and complete daily tasks.
4. Track personal habits.
5. Add courses and academic deadlines.
6. View upcoming academic responsibilities.
7. Have an AI conversation.
8. Control their personal information and privacy settings.

## 8. Scope Management

Features outside the MVP should not delay the first working release.

New features should be evaluated based on student value, technical complexity, privacy impact, and development effort.
