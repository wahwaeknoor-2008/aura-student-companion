# AURA — Product Vision

## What is AURA?
AURA is an AI-powered student life companion designed to help university and college students manage independent student life. It connects academic organization, healthy routines, practical life guidance, and supportive AI conversations.

## The problem
Students balance coursework, deadlines, health habits, everyday responsibilities, social relationships, and the transition to independent living. Existing tools often address these needs separately.

## Vision
Make student life easier to organize and navigate while helping students become more confident, capable, and connected.

## Product pillars
1. **AI companion:** helpful conversations, student-approved context, and supportive check-ins.
2. **Academic life:** courses, assignments, exams, calendars, and study planning.
3. **Daily habits:** hydration, meals, sleep, exercise, and flexible progress tracking.
4. **Everyday assistance:** practical step-by-step guidance for cooking, laundry, budgeting, and other life skills.
5. **AURA Live:** future opt-in voice and camera guidance.

## Product principles
- Student-first and useful.
- Encourage independence rather than dependence.
- Transparent, student-controlled memory.
- Privacy by design.
- No guilt-based or manipulative gamification.
- Encourage healthy human connection and professional support when appropriate.

## Target users
University and college students, especially those living away from family or adjusting to independent living.

## Long-term direction
Evolve from a student productivity and wellbeing app into a privacy-conscious student life platform with optional campus, calendar, wearable, and on-device AI integrations.
